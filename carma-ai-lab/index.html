CARMA LAB

Clinical AI for Reliable Medical Analytics

Reliable AI for high-stakes decisions.

About

CARMA Lab was founded by Md. Kawsher Mahbub to build clinical AI that earns its place in real medical practice. The lab works on the problems that stand between a model that performs and a model that can be deployed: rigorous evaluation and auditing, generalization under distribution shift, interpretability, and uncertainty quantification.

The goal is medical analytics that clinicians can understand, verify, and trust when the cost of being wrong is real.

Research

Evaluation & Auditing

We probe models for failure before deployment does, stress-testing classifiers the way regulators should.

Representative work: Forensic explainability auditing of ensemble chest X-ray classifiers.

Generalization

Benchmark accuracy is not hospital accuracy. We study out-of-distribution generalization across sites, scanners, and populations.

Representative work: STAMP, predicting OOD generalization without target data.

Interpretability

Clinicians should see why a model decided what it did. We build explanation methods grounded in clinical practice.

Representative work: Explainable AI for chest radiographs and heart disease diagnosis.

Safety & Uncertainty

A model that knows when it is uncertain is safer than one that is merely accurate.

Representative work: SpatialUQ, post-hoc uncertainty quantification from spatial consistency in black-box vision models.
